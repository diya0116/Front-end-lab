Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in Campus Fest 2026?

College students who meet the eligibility requirements for an event can participate in Campus Fest 2026.

How can I register for an event?

You can register by visiting the Register page and filling out the participant registration form.

Can I participate in more than one event?

Yes, you can participate in multiple events as long as their schedules do not clash.

Is there a registration fee?

Registration for Campus Fest 2026 is currently free for all participants.

Will participants receive certificates?

Yes, all registered participants will receive a participation certificate after the event.
